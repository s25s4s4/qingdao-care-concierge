Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa to visit Qingdao for treatment?

Citizens of most European countries (including Germany, France, Italy, Switzerland, the Netherlands, Spain), Singapore, Malaysia, and others currently enjoy a 15-day visa-free entry to China. For travelers requiring a visa, our partner hospitals issue official medical appointment invitation letters to make tourist/business visa processing seamless.

How do I verify the authenticity of dental implants?

Every patient receives the original sealed implant packaging box, serial number, and the official Straumann / Nobel Biocare Global Warranty Card with a verifiable QR code. Any dentist worldwide can service and inspect the genuine component.

Will the doctors and nurses speak English?

Yes. The International Wings at QDUH and Municipal Hospital feature doctors who trained in the US, Europe, or South Korea. In addition, Qingdao Care assigns you a dedicated bilingual care coordinator who stays with you throughout diagnostics, surgery, and city tours.
