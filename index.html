Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I pay the hospital directly instead of paying an all-inclusive fee?

We believe in total financial transparency. By paying the public university hospital cashier directly, you ensure there are zero hidden commissions, inflated surgery markups, or third-party fees. You receive official state medical invoices and inspect your unsealed genuine hardware yourself.

What exactly does the $1,200 concierge fee cover?

Our flat fee covers your dedicated 1-on-1 English medical coordinator throughout your trip, priority VIP appointment fast-tracking with chief surgeons, private chauffeured airport transfers (both ways), all door-to-door transport for clinic visits, a high-speed China 5G SIM card, and expedited English clinical dossiers.

Can I choose my own hotel?

Absolutely. You are welcome to book any hotel you prefer via Booking.com or Expedia, or choose from our partner beachfront hotels (ranging from $50/night comfortable 4-star oceanfront hotels to 5-star international luxury such as Hyatt Regency). Our private chauffeur will pick you up from your doorstep regardless of where you stay.

Do I have to do the sightseeing tour?

Not at all. The tour is 100% optional. Many patients prefer to simply complete their medical procedure and rest peacefully by the seaside. If you do wish to explore Qingdao's famous German heritage and coastal scenery during your recovery days, we arrange a gentle, guided itinerary tailored to your pace.
