Implantology & Bone Science

1. Swiss Straumann SLA vs. SLActive (Hydrophilic)

At QDUH Dental Implant Center (the designated chair unit of Qingdao Stomatological Society), Straumann SLA (sandblasted large-grit acid-etched pure titanium) is the standard workhorse for healthy bone with a 6–8 week osseointegration period. For patients with mild diabetes, smoking history, or compromised bone density, surgeons utilize SLActive (chemically active hydrophilic surface), which accelerates blood protein absorption and shortens bone integration to 3–4 weeks.

2. When is Bone Grafting (GBR) Truly Necessary?

A genuine dental implant requires at least 1.5mm to 2mm of healthy labial bone thickness surrounding the titanium fixture. If a tooth has been missing for years, disuse atrophy causes horizontal or vertical bone resorption. If initial 3D CBCT reveals bone width < 5.5mm, surgeons perform Guided Bone Regeneration (GBR) using Swiss Geistlich Bio-Oss bovine bone mineral + Bio-Gide collagen membrane to ensure decades of stability.

3. Maxillary Sinus Floor Elevation (Internal vs. External)

In the upper posterior molar area, maxillary sinus pneumatization is common. If residual alveolar bone height is 5mm–8mm, an internal crestal sinus lift (Summers osteotome technique) is completed simultaneously during implant drilling. If residual bone is < 4mm, an external lateral window sinus elevation is performed with Piezo-electric ultrasonic bone surgery.

👁️

Refractive Laser & Corneal Safety

1. Strict Eligibility: Who is Disqualified from SMILE?

QDUH Eye Hospital (founded in 1902, national ophthalmology doctoral center) strictly enforces safety cutoff lines:
• Central Corneal Thickness (CCT): Must be ≥ 480 μm.
• Residual Stromal Bed (RSB): Must remain strictly > 280 μm (safety threshold against ectasia).
• Forme Fruste Keratoconus: Any abnormal posterior elevation on Pentacam tomography is permanently disqualified.
• Correction Range: Up to -10.00D myopia and -5.00D astigmatism.

2. Why SMILE Outperforms Traditional LASIK for Travelers

Unlike traditional LASIK which cuts a 20mm circular flap, the German Carl Zeiss VisuMax femtosecond laser creates an intra-stromal refractive lenticule extracted through a microscopic 2mm to 4mm arc incision. Because anterior corneal nerves and biomechanical tensile strength remain 80% intact, post-op dry eye syndrome is dramatically lower and the risk of traumatic flap dislocation during active travel is 0%.

3. Post-Operative Air Travel Protocol

Can you fly after laser eye surgery? Yes. Because the 2mm micro-incision self-seals within 4–6 hours, patients are cleared for commercial cabin flights on Day 3 post-op. We supply a hospital-prescribed sterile artificial tear regimen (preservative-free Sodium Hyaluronate) to counteract low cabin humidity.

Diagnostic Radiology & Endoscopy

1. 3.0T High-Field MRI vs. Standard 1.5T MRI

QDUH operates Siemens and GE 3.0-Tesla high-field superconducting magnetic resonance scanners. Compared to standard 1.5T scanners prevalent in budget centers, 3.0T doubles the signal-to-noise ratio, enabling sub-millimeter spatial resolution capable of detecting micro-ischemic cerebrovascular lesions, early unruptured intracranial aneurysms, and subtle spinal disc nerve compressions.

2. Sedated Painless Endoscopy & Immediate Polypectomy

Under Propofol intravenous anesthesia monitored by board-certified anesthesiologists, both gastroscopy and colonoscopy are performed in a single 25-minute comfortable sleep state. Utilizing Olympus high-magnification Narrow Band Imaging (NBI), suspicious mucosal lesions are enhanced. If benign adenomatous polyps (< 10mm) are identified, endoscopic electroresection (polypectomy) is performed immediately on-site with histopathological biopsy report provided.

3. International Transferability of DICOM Imaging

All radiological datasets are burned onto uncompressed DICOM format discs accompanied by bilingual radiologist interpretations (English/Chinese). These discs conform to universal PACS interoperability and can be handed directly to your home GP or specialist in the UK, US, or Germany with 100% diagnostic validity.
